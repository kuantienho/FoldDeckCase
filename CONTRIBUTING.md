# Contributing

Build reports are welcome. Include the file version, printer and nozzle, material, changed settings, actual print time, card and sleeve dimensions, and photos of the flat print and assembled case.

For a problem report, describe where assembly becomes difficult, which latch or fold is involved, and whether whitening, cracking, or misalignment appears. Note approximately how many opening cycles occurred.

For a proposed design change, explain the problem it solves and include a dimensioned comparison. Keep decorative artwork separate from the Basic model and only submit material you have permission to share.

## Contribution permissions

Identify the source and license of any submitted changes. Contributions remain subject to their stated license. Submitting a contribution does not automatically grant the creator separate commercial rights; those permissions will be discussed explicitly if needed.
