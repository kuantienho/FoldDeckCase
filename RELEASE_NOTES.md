# FoldDeckCase Basic v1.5

A thin, single-piece card carrier that prints flat and folds into shape.

- One plate, no separate assembly hardware, and no supports for Basic.
- Seven-layer A1 profile: approximately 1 h 33 min and 40.79 g in the slicer.
- Editable STEP model and Bambu Studio 3MF project included.
- Extended and rounded slit roots on all three latches, with resized flexible patches.

Open the 3MF as a project and check your printer, plate, and material before slicing. Keep the saved orientation and scale.

Earlier prototypes were printed and assembled successfully. Physical testing of the latest v1.5 latch changes is pending. This release should be marked as a prerelease until that check is complete.
